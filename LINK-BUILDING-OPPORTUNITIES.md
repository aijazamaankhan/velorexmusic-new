# Link-building and digital PR — velorexmusic.com

The aim is **editorial mentions from sites and people who genuinely care about
physical music in India** — links that also send customers. One relevant
mention from a music writer is worth more than any number of directory links,
and it cannot get the site penalised.

Named outlets below are **examples to research**, not endorsements and not
contacts that have been made. Check each one is active and a fit before
reaching out.

## Never do these

- Buying links, "guest posts" on paid networks, or link exchanges
- Private blog networks (PBNs) or automated backlink tools
- Mass directory submissions or "SEO packages" promising N backlinks
- Comment spam on blogs, forums or YouTube
- Fake reviews, fake testimonials or paid review swaps

Google treats all of these as link spam. The downside (a manual action on the
whole domain) is far larger than any upside.

## How to approach people

1. Read and understand their work first; pitch only what fits it.
2. Offer something useful to *their* readers — a guide, a quote, an image, a
   fact from real Velorex data — not a request for a link.
3. One personal email; one polite follow-up after a week; then stop.
4. Keep a simple log (who, date, what was offered, outcome).
5. Disclose any gift or payment clearly. Influencer content that is paid or
   gifted must be labelled as such.

## Easy wins that are not outreach

- **Google Business Profile** for both stores (name, address and phone matching
  `contact.html` exactly).
- **Discogs seller profile**, if you sell there, linking to the site.
- Real **profiles** on the social platforms already in the Organization schema
  (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp channel), each linking back.
- Being genuinely helpful in collector communities under the shop's name —
  answers, not advertising.

## 10 linkable-content ideas

Each is something a writer could cite. **No statistics are to be invented** —
anything numeric must come from Velorex's own data or a named source.

| # | Idea | What it is | Built from | Who would link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Collector's Guide to Bollywood Vinyl** | The cluster-1 pillar article, the definitive practical guide | Catalogue + verified history | Bollywood writers, collector groups |
| 2 | **Vinyl Pressings Explained** ✅ | Already written — editions, 180g, colour, picture discs | Catalogue examples | Collector communities, hi-fi forums |
| 3 | **Storing Records in India's Climate** ✅ | Already written — the monsoon angle is unique to India | General care practice | Audio forums, lifestyle/home writers |
| 4 | **The Vinyl Grading Cheat Sheet** | A one-page printable/shareable graphic of grades (M to P) | Standard grading terms | Collector groups, sellers |
| 5 | **Hindi Film Music on Vinyl: A Timeline** | Decades, composers and the reissues available now | The existing Music History section + catalogue | Culture sites, educators |
| 6 | **What Indian Vinyl Buyers Actually Buy** | Anonymised, aggregated Velorex sales data: most-bought decades, composers, formats | **Real Velorex orders only**, published once there is enough data to be meaningful | Music journalists (data stories get cited) |
| 7 | **The R. D. Burman Vinyl Guide** ✅ | Already written — plus a shareable "where to start" graphic | Catalogue track listings | Fan communities, film-music writers |
| 8 | **Anniversary pieces** | "Sholay at 50: the soundtrack on vinyl" and similar dated stories | Catalogue + verified film history | Film press at the anniversary |
| 9 | **Starter Crate** | "Ten records to begin a Bollywood vinyl collection", with reasons | Catalogue | Gift guides, beginner communities |
| 10 | **Owner interview / behind the counter** | How records are checked, packed and shipped across India | Real Velorex process | Local press, podcasts |

Publish each as a Journal article (so it builds the site's authority, not a
PDF elsewhere), then point outreach at it.
