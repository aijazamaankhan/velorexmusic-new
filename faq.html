Frequently Asked Questions

Are all vinyl records new?
Unless specified otherwise as "Pre-owned" or "Vintage", all our vinyl records are brand new, factory-sealed, and sourced directly from reputable labels and distributors.
How do you ensure records don't break during shipping?
We use specialized, double-walled cardboard mailers designed specifically for vinyl. We also include stiffeners to prevent bending and ensure the corners remain sharp.
What is a 180g Vinyl?
180g refers to the weight of the vinyl disc. Heavier vinyl is generally more durable and less prone to warping over time, often providing a higher quality playback experience.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?
Yes, we offer COD for most pin codes across India for orders up to ₹5,000. For higher amounts, we recommend using secure prepaid methods.
Can I cancel my order?
You can cancel your order within 12 hours of placing it, as long as it hasn't been dispatched. Once dispatched, you will need to follow our return policy.
