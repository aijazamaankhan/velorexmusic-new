Spin & Win: Terms and Conditions

These rules apply to the Spin & Win welcome game on velorexmusic.com. Playing the game means you accept them.

1. The organiser

The Spin & Win welcome game ("the Game") is run by Velorex Music ("we", "us"). Questions: orders@velorexmusic.com.

2. Who can play

  • You must be 18 or over and live in India.
  • You need a free Velorex Music account. New members, and existing members who have never completed an order, can play.
  • Customers who have already completed an order, as a member or as a guest with the same email, cannot play.
  • One play per person. One account gets one play, whichever game is shown. We also limit plays from one device and one internet connection.
  • No purchase is necessary to play.

3. How the result is decided

When you play, our system picks your result at random using the chances listed under Current prizes and chances below. The wheel, reels, scratch card, gift box, record or envelope only reveals a result that has already been decided; how you play does not change it. The result recorded by our system is final.

4. Prizes and chances

The prizes on offer, their conditions and the chance of winning each one are listed under Current prizes and chances below. That list is generated from the live game settings, so it always shows the current chances.

5. Using your prize

  • A winning result gives you a coupon code, shown on screen and emailed to your account's address.
  • The code is valid for the number of days stated below, can be used once, and only on the account that won it.
  • Apply it at checkout. One coupon per order; it cannot be combined with other coupons or offers.
  • Any minimum order value or minimum number of vinyl LPs stated for the prize must be met by the items in the cart, before delivery.
  • Percentage and rupee discounts apply to items only, not to delivery. "Free Delivery" waives the delivery charge on one order shipped within India.
  • Prizes have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for cash, transferred, resold, or replaced if they expire unused.

6. Returns and cancellations

If an order that used a code is cancelled or returned, we refund the amount you actually paid. A used code is not reissued, except where we cancelled the order ourselves.

7. Fair play

Creating several accounts, using false details or automated tools, or any other attempt to play more than once or to influence the result is not allowed. If that happens we may cancel the code and any unshipped order placed with it, and refund what was paid.

8. Mistakes and technical problems

If a technical fault shows a result different from the one our system recorded, the recorded result applies and we will tell you. If the Game fails before a result is recorded, you have not used your play and may try again.

9. Changing or ending the Game

We may change the prizes, the chances or the game, or end the Game, at any time. Codes already won keep the terms they were issued with until they expire.

10. Your information

We use your account details to run the Game and email your code, as described in our Privacy Policy. We only send marketing email if you ticked the box asking for it, and every such email has an unsubscribe link.

11. Our responsibility

Nothing in these rules limits your rights under Indian consumer protection law. Apart from that, our responsibility is to provide the prize you won as described here.

12. Law

These rules are governed by the laws of India, and disputes are subject to the courts of Gurugram, Haryana. Please contact us first; most problems are quicker to fix by email.
