## Suggested description structure

Use only the sections you have real information for:

1. **What the record is** — film or album, year of the film, director or lead
   cast if relevant (one or two sentences).
2. **The music** — composer, lyricist, singers; the two or three songs people
   come for.
3. **This pressing** — label, reissue or original, edition/run, vinyl weight
   and colour, gatefold or single sleeve, inserts, number of discs.
4. **Condition** (pre-owned only) — disc and sleeve separately, any marks,
   whether play-tested.
5. **Why collectors want it** — only if there is a genuine reason (first
   edition, limited colour, landmark score).

Aim for 80–200 words. The track listing belongs in the track-listing field,
not the description.
